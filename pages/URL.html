Uniform Resource Locator (URL)

A URL is the address of a resource on the web.

PartMeaningExample
SchemeProtocolhttps
HostDomain namewww.example.com
PathFile location/about/page.html
QueryData sent to server?id=10

Key idea: each part of the URL gives the browser a navigation clue.
