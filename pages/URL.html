URL Structure

A URL (Uniform Resource Locator) is the address of a webpage or resource on the Internet.

ComponentMeaningExample
ProtocolHow to access the resourcehttps://
DomainWebsite addressexample.com
PathLocation of page on server/pages/about
QuerySearch parameters?id=123

Key idea: A URL tells the browser where to find a resource and how to fetch it.
