Intranet & Extranet

An intranet is a private internal network for one organization. An extranet gives limited outside access to trusted users.

TermAccessUse
IntranetEmployees onlyInternal documents and portals
ExtranetSelected outsidersPartner access and shared systems

Key idea: intranet = inside only, extranet = controlled outside access.
